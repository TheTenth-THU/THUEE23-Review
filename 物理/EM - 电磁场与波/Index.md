## Course Knowledge Map, 课程知识地图

Electromagnetism connects sources, fields, forces, energy, and waves. The course begins with vector analysis, builds electrostatic and magnetostatic field laws from charge and current, introduces polarization and magnetization in matter, and then combines induction with Maxwell's addition to obtain the full time-varying theory. Wave equations, radiation, propagation in media, and boundary phenomena follow from that unified framework.

## Knowledge Navigation, 知识点导航

+ **[[Math Basement]]**: vector algebra, gradient, divergence, curl, line and surface integrals, Stokes' theorem, and Gauss' theorem used throughout the field equations.
+ **[[Electrostatics]]**: the electrostatic problem chain from Coulomb's law and superposition to Gauss' law, potential, uniqueness, conductors, dielectrics, and the method of images.
    + **[[Electric Fields]]**: electric-field definitions, flux laws, and the circuital law for static fields.
    + **[[Electric Potential]]**: conservative fields, potential, Poisson and Laplace equations, and boundary-value methods.
    + **[[Electric Displacement]]**: polarization, bound charge, the auxiliary field $\v{D}$, constitutive relations, boundary conditions, and capacitors.
+ **[[Magnetostatics]]**: the magnetic counterpart built from steady currents, Lorentz force, Biot–Savart and Ampère laws, magnetic dipoles, and magnetic media.
    + **[[Magnetic Fields]]**: current densities, the field $\v{B}$, magnetic flux, vector potential, and typical current distributions.
    + **[[Magnetic Vector Potential]]**: gauge freedom, representative distributions, and the Aharonov–Bohm effect.
    + **[[Magnetic Auxiliary Field H]]**: magnetization, bound current, the auxiliary field $\v{H}$, constitutive relations, and magnetic boundary conditions.
+ **[[Electromagnetic Induction]]**: Faraday induction and Maxwell's displacement-current correction bridge static fields to time-varying fields.
+ **[[Maxwell's Equations]]**: integrates the four field equations with Lorentz force, electromagnetic energy, and Poynting's theorem.
+ **[[Electromagnetic Waves]]**: derives wave equations and plane-wave structure from Maxwell's equations.
    + **[[Electromagnetic Radiation]]**: retarded potentials, dipole radiation, angular distribution, and near- and far-field limits.
    + **[[Electromagnetic Waves in Media]]**: refractive index, dispersion, group velocity, loss, and material response.
    + **[[Electromagnetic Waves at Boundaries]]**: Snell and Fresnel relations, polarization, Brewster angle, total internal reflection, and guided modes.
